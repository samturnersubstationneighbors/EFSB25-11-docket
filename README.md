# EFSB 25-11 Docket

Copies of public documents from the Massachusetts Energy Facilities Siting Board
(EFSB) docket 25-11 (Falmouth Tap Station Replacement Project).

> [!NOTE]
> Documents included here are copies and are provided for convenience. They
> should be verified against the original version in the
> [official EFSB 25-11 docket](https://eeaonline.eea.state.ma.us/dpu/fileroom/#/dockets/docket/12774).
> This repository is not affiliated with the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, the
> EFSB, or the Department of Public Utilities.
